Cell phone & distracted driving

CPC Logistics Canada · Revised 2025

Your safety and the public's safety come first. Handheld device use while operating a commercial motor vehicle is prohibited — in Ontario and everywhere CPC operates, including cross-border runs.

  • No handheld calls, texting, or app use while the vehicle is in motion.
  • Hands-free is permitted only where lawful and only when it doesn't distract from driving.
  • Program routes and messages before you roll, or while safely parked.
  • Violations may result in progressive discipline, up to termination.

If you ever feel pressured to use a device while driving, stop safely and call dispatch. We will always back the safe choice.

Stop 3 of 5 · Safety training

Workplace violence & harassment

Section 2 of 3. This is a required OHSA training module — not just a signature. Read the short brief, then answer one quick check.

▲ Required training · OHSA

Everyone has the right to a safe, respectful workplace

Workplace violence is any use — or threat — of physical force that could injure you. Harassment is unwelcome conduct that a person knows, or should know, is unwelcome. Both are covered by Ontario's Occupational Health and Safety Act, and CPC has a program to prevent them.

As a driver you work alone and off-site, so knowing how to recognize, avoid, and report incidents matters. Reporting is confidential and CPC cannot penalize you for raising a concern in good faith.
